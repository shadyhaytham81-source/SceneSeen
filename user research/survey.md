# Survey: how you watch, and what you do when you like something on screen

About 5 minutes. Build it in Google Forms or Microsoft Forms. Put the consent text (`consent_form.md`, short version)
on the first page, with a required "I agree to take part" box before any questions.

Notes for building the form:
- Keep the question order: habits first, then the "I liked something" moment, then SceneSeen-specific questions.
  Asking about SceneSeen early biases the earlier answers.
- Don't describe SceneSeen until section 4.
- Contact details are only collected in section 5, and only if someone opts in.
