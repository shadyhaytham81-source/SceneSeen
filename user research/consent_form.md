## Full version (interviews and tests)

**Project:** SceneSeen, an app that finds the clothes and other products in scenes from series and films.
**Team:** Adel Nabih, Noureen Heikal, Mohamed Essam, Shady El Haytham, Habiba Wael, Jana Safir.
**Supervisor:** Dr. Mohammed Tharwat.
**University:** ESLSCA University, Computing & Digital Technology.
**Contact:** [team contact email].

### What you'll do
[Choose one:]
- **Interview:** a conversation of about 20–30 minutes about how you watch series and films, and what you do when you
  notice something you like.
- **Concept test:** watching a short clip and trying two ways of finding the products in it (pointing your phone at
  the screen, and scanning a QR code), then answering a few questions. About 20 minutes.
- **Usability test:** using the SceneSeen app to complete a few tasks, then filling in a short questionnaire. About
  20–30 minutes.

### Recording
[Interview / tests:] With your permission, we'll take notes and may record the audio or screen, only so we don't miss
anything. Recordings are kept by the team, never shared, and deleted when the project ends ([month, year]).

### What we collect and why
- Your answers and observations, to understand how people watch and to improve the app.
- Your name and contact details, only to arrange the session. These are stored separately from your answers.
- We don't collect ID numbers or any information we don't need.

### How it's used
- Results appear in our graduation project report and presentations only as patterns and totals
  (e.g. "9 of 12 participants watch Ramadan series on TV").
- We may quote something you said, without your name or anything that identifies you.

### Your rights
- Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask us to delete your answers and recordings until [date the analysis is finished]. After that, answers are
  already combined anonymously and can't be separated.
- Questions or requests: [team contact email].
